# Planto 🌱

Planto is an iOS app that helps you take care of your plants.. It keeps all your plant reminders in one place and notifies you when it's time to water them

## Main Features

- **Onboarding:** a welcome screen shown only the first time the app is opened.
- **Add plant reminders:** set the plant name, room, light, watering days, and water amount.
- **Today's list:** view all your plants and check each one off after watering it.
- **Progress tracking:** a progress bar and message show how many plants you have cared for.
- **Completion screen:** an "All Done!" screen appears when every reminder is completed.
- **Edit and delete:** tap a plant to edit its details, or swipe it to delete.
- **Notifications:** get a local notification reminding you to water each plant, based on its watering days.
- **Saved data:** your plants stay saved after you close the app.

## Technologies Used

- Swift and SwiftUI
- MVVM architecture
- UserNotifications (local notifications)
- UserDefaults with Codable (JSON) for saving data
- Xcode
